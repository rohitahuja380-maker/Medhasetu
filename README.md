# Medha Setu

## Smart Learning Platform for Students

Medha Setu is a smart learning website designed to help students study in a more effective and organized way. The main goal of this project is to make learning easier by using modern web technologies and AI-based features.

Website: https://medha-setu.ai.studio

## About the Project

Students often have difficulty finding the right study resources, understanding difficult topics, and managing their study time. Medha Setu is built to provide a simple digital platform where students can learn, practice, and improve their understanding of different subjects.

The platform focuses on making studying more interactive and accessible while reducing the difficulty students face during self-study.

## Features

* Smart learning support for students
* AI-powered assistance for learning
* Simple and user-friendly interface
* Helps students understand difficult topics
* Supports better and more organized study
* Web-based platform that can be accessed from a browser
* Designed with students and their learning needs in mind

## Who Can Use Medha Setu?

Medha Setu can be useful for:

* School students
* College students
* Students preparing for exams
* Anyone who wants a simple platform for self-learning

## Vision

The idea behind Medha Setu is to use technology to make education simpler and more accessible.

The goal is not just to provide another learning website, but to create a platform that can support students throughout their learning journey.
